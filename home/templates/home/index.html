This Wiki serves as an encyclopaedia for the three core Jak and Daxter games:

  • Jak and Daxter: The Precursor Legacy (2001)
  • Jak II (2003)
  • Jak 3 (2004)

The series evolves from colourful platforming to gritty, story-driven action:

Jak and Daxter: The Precursor Legacy

A seamless-world 3D platformer where Jak and his ottsel-transformed friend Daxter collect Power Cells to restore Daxter and stop Gol and Maia from unleashing Dark Eco.

Jak II

Introduces the Morph Gun, a dystopian Haven City, and Jak’s Dark Eco powers. Inspired by GTA-style world design, Jak and Daxter fight tyrants, Metal Heads, and their own fate through time.

Jak 3

Adds eight new Morph Gun mods, Light Jak abilities, and a vast desert Wasteland. This game concludes the trilogy with answers about the Precursors, the Metal Heads, and Jak’s destiny.
